CareFlow – Smart Hospital Management System

CareFlow is a hospital management system designed to simplify daily healthcare operations by connecting patients, doctors, receptionists, laboratory staff, pharmacists and administrators through one centralized platform.

About CareFlow

CareFlow was developed to address common operational challenges encountered in modern healthcare environments, including fragmented medical records, disjointed scheduling across departments, delayed diagnostic report dissemination, and uncoordinated medicine dispensing.

By unifying front-desk registration, clinical consultation notes, laboratory workflows, prescription tracking, and billing into an integrated system, CareFlow ensures that medical professionals have access to verified patient data when they need it most.

Core Implemented Functionality

1. Patient Management

Self-registration, profile management, medical appointment tracking, prescription history, and personal lab results access.

2. Appointment Management

Real-time appointment booking, automated physician assignment based on clinical symptoms, and live consultation status tracking.

3. Doctor Consultations

Clinician appointment schedule management, consultation review, electronic prescription authoring, and direct diagnostic test requests.

4. Laboratory Diagnostics

Diagnostic test order intake, sample tracking, clinical result value entry, and verified diagnostic PDF report generation.

5. Pharmacy Inventory

Pharmaceutical catalog maintenance, stock level tracking, automated stock reduction upon dispense, and patient medicine purchases.

6. Billing & Receipts

Transparent fee collection for consultations, medicine purchases, and diagnostic tests with itemized payment receipts.

7. Six-Tier Role Management

Granular role-based access control safeguarding patient information across Administrators, Doctors, Receptionists, Lab Staff, Pharmacists, and Patients.

How CareFlow Works

  1. Patient Registration: Patients create a secure verified account with real-time OTP confirmation.
  2. Appointment Booking: Patient selects their health problem and is scheduled with an appropriate specialist.
  3. Doctor Consultation: The physician examines the patient, records clinical notes, and writes prescriptions or requests lab tests.
  4. Laboratory Testing: Lab technicians collect samples, record quantitative results, and finalize diagnostic reports.
  5. Pharmacy Dispense: Pharmacist fulfills validated prescriptions and inventory automatically reflects dispensed items.
  6. Billing & Payment: Front desk or online checkout processes transparent billing with verified payment tracking.

User Roles & Responsibilities

  • Patients: Book appointments, review prescriptions, view lab reports, purchase prescribed medicines, and submit physician feedback.
  • Doctors: View assigned patients, manage daily clinical queues, record diagnoses, order lab tests, and author prescriptions.
  • Receptionists: Onboard walk-in patients, manage front-desk appointment queues, process consultations, and collect payments.
  • Laboratory Staff: Process incoming test orders, record diagnostic metrics, and publish verified laboratory reports.
  • Pharmacists: Maintain medicine catalog, verify prescription orders, dispense pharmaceuticals, and track stock reorder thresholds.
  • Administrators: Monitor hospital operational metrics, audit user accounts, supervise pharmacy inventory, and manage system logs.
